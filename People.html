Dr. Xue Pan

Assistant Professor

My research has always centred on plant lipid biology, a path that has taken me from the field to the cell membrane. During my MSc at Dalhousie University, I evaluated the agronomic performance of oilseed crops. For my PhD at the University of Alberta, I turned to what happens inside the seed, studying how flax produces its oil. As an NSERC Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of California, Riverside, I went smaller still, exploring how membrane nanodomains organize signaling in plant cells.
